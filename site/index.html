Privacy

Every number is worked out on your Mac.

Exactly what it reads, and the only two times anything goes out.

Read on your Mac

  • Codex logs in ~/.codex/sessions
  • Claude Code's own logs, and the quota it passes to its status line (if you choose that source)
  • The process list, memory and open ports of agent sessions

Sent only if you choose

  • Quota from the vendors: each login token goes only to its own vendor (api.anthropic.com, chatgpt.com), every 5 minutes
  • The update check: asks GitHub for the latest release when you press the button

Never

  • An account, analytics, or a TokenPet server
  • Refreshing or writing your login tokens: they are read-only
  • Closing anything without your click, unless you turn on auto-close (5-minute warning, with a Keep button)

Install

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VoTuTrinh/tokenpet/main/install.sh | sh
  1. Download and drag to ApplicationsOr paste the command above into Terminal: it downloads, installs and opens the app.
  2. First launchThis build is not notarized by Apple yet. If macOS blocks it: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. The command above avoids this step.

Questions

Before you install.

My Mac has no notch.

TokenPet shows as a small tab on the edge of the screen instead, by itself. Drag it up or down; click it to open. You can switch to a menu bar item in Settings.

Will it close my sessions on its own?

No. Auto-close is off by default; if you turn it on, a 5-minute warning with a Keep button comes first.

Why does macOS ask for Keychain access?

Only if you choose to ask the servers for your quota. TokenPet reads the login Claude Code saved, to ask Anthropic about your own usage. Choose Always Allow to be asked once.

I pay per token through the API.

Then the quota half is not for you: TokenPet tracks subscription limits (Claude Pro and Max, ChatGPT Plus and Pro), not bills. The memory half works either way.

Is it free?

Yes, and the source is on GitHub. English and Vietnamese; it follows your Mac's language, and you can pin one in Settings.
